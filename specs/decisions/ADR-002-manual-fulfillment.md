# ADR-002: Manual courier fulfillment for MVP

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-11

## Decision

Use RajaOngkir to search destinations and quote shipping during checkout.
After payment, staff books the shipment manually and records courier and airway
bill in the dashboard.

## Consequences

The MVP does not require RajaOngkir Shipping Delivery Enterprise operations.
Automated booking, pickup, label generation, and tracking synchronization need
a later approved specification.
